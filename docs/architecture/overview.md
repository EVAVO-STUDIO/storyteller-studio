# Architecture

Storyteller Studio uses a provider-independent and provenance-preserving
production pipeline.

## Manuscript layer

Imports source material without silently changing it. Each import receives an
immutable content fingerprint and revision record.

## Story intelligence layer

Produces proposed chapters, scenes, dialogue attribution, characters,
locations, terminology, emotional beats and continuity records. Proposed
knowledge remains distinguishable from author-approved canon.

## Performance direction layer

Converts narrative intent into explicit direction covering:

- narrator stance and narrative distance;
- emotional objective;
- energy and intensity;
- pace and phrase shape;
- breath and pause placement;
- emphasis and de-emphasis;
- character intention;
- subtext;
- scene transitions;
- pronunciation;
- continuity references.

## Generation layer

Routes approved performance segments through replaceable voice providers.
Every take records its provider, model, voice, configuration, input fingerprint
and production context.

## Quality layer

Checks missing or altered text, pronunciation, clipping, noise, timing,
silence, cadence repetition, discontinuity, character drift and loudness.
Automated findings support rather than replace human creative approval.

## Production layer

Assembles approved takes, chapter markers, room tone, transitions, metadata and
delivery files.

## Visual storytelling layer

Creates an optional visual script derived from the approved manuscript and
performance plan. It preserves character, location, costume, period, colour and
composition continuity across the work.

## Durable project knowledge

Projects preserve source revisions, story-bible revisions, pronunciation
decisions, voice profiles, performance plans, generated takes, reviews,
approvals, quality findings, mastering settings, export manifests and rights
records.

Generated media is replaceable. Approved project knowledge and production
history are durable.
